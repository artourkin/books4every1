## Sample library

The optional library consists of short excerpts, not complete works, from Walden (1854), Pride and Prejudice (1813), Moby-Dick (1851), The Secret Garden (1911), Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (1865), and Frankenstein (1818). These texts are public domain in the United States; verify local public-domain status if redistributing elsewhere. No Project Gutenberg branding or editions are bundled. Sample covers are original CSS artwork made for this project.
